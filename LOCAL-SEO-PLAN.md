# ShilpSmith — Plan to Rank in Bareilly (Local SEO)

Written in plain language. Nothing has been changed in the code yet.

---

## The one thing to understand first

When someone in Bareilly searches **"3d printing near me"**, Google shows **two different things** on one page:

**1. The map box (top)** — 3 businesses with a map, stars, phone number, "Directions" button.
This comes from **Google Business Profile**. It does **NOT** come from your website.
Most people tap here.

**2. Normal blue links (below)**
This comes from **your website**.

Right now we have only worked on #2. That is why the site is in Google but nobody finds it.
**We must do #1 first.** Without it, we can never appear in the map box, no matter how good the website is.

---

## Why the site is not ranking yet — the real reasons

1. **No Google Business Profile.** So we are invisible in the map box.
2. **No page for most keywords.** Google can only rank a page that exists. We have a homepage, a shop page, 6 category pages, and product pages. There is **no page** about "3D printing services", "keychains", "pen stands", "earphone organiser", or "couple gifts". So we cannot rank for them.
3. **The city name "Bareilly" appears almost nowhere.** Google has no reason to think we are a Bareilly business.
4. **The website is brand new.** No other website links to us yet. Google trusts new sites slowly. This takes months, not days.
5. **Possible technical issue to check** — see Step 0 below.

## The plan — 5 steps, in order

### Step 0 — Quick check (15 minutes, do today)

Two things to confirm before anything else:

**a) Check the website address setting on Vercel.**
There is a setting called `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`. It must be exactly:
```
https://shilpsmith.com
```
This has gone wrong before on this project — it was once pointing to a different domain. If it is wrong, **every page is telling Google to index a website that does not exist**. This alone would stop all ranking. Please check it.

**b) In Google Search Console, open "Pages".**
Look at the number under **"Indexed"** (not "Discovered" or "Crawled").
- If it says 30–40 → good, indexing is fine, move to Step 1.
- If it says 2–5 → Google has found the site but is not accepting most pages. Tell me the number and I will investigate.

### Step 1 — Google Business Profile (THIS WEEK — most important)

**I cannot do this. You must do it.** It needs your phone, your identity, and Google's verification.

Go to **business.google.com** and create a free profile.

**Fill it like this:**

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Business name | `ShilpSmith 3D Studio` (exactly this, nothing extra) |
| Main category | `3D printing service` |
| Extra categories | `Gift shop`, `Souvenir store` |
| Address | Choose **"I deliver goods to customers"** and **hide the address** (it is your home) |
| Service area | Bareilly, Rampur, Shahjahanpur, Pilibhit, Badaun |
| Phone | `+91 82799 84069` (exactly the same as on the website) |
| Website | `https://shilpsmith.com` |
| Hours | Your real working hours |

Google will send a postcard or ask for a video call to verify. This takes 5–14 days. **Start now** because everything else waits on this.

**Then, every week:**
- Upload real photos of your products (10–20 to start, then 2–3 per week)
- Post an update (new product, offer, festival gift idea)

**Most important of all — Google reviews.**
Reviews on Google are the **number one reason** one business appears above another in the map box.

Important: the reviews already on our website **do not count** for this. They are separate.

- Ask every customer to leave a Google review after delivery
- Send the review link on WhatsApp with the delivery message
- Ask them to mention what they bought — e.g. "beautiful custom keychain" — because those words help us rank for "keychain"
- **Target: 10 reviews in the first month.** In Bareilly, 10 genuine reviews is often enough to reach the top 3.

### Step 5 — Keep adding content (ongoing, 2–4 articles per month)

The site has no blog. A blog is how a new store gets its first visitors, because it can rank for smaller, easier searches:

- "Gift ideas for boyfriend under ₹1000"
- "How are 3D printed miniatures made?"
- "Best return gifts for weddings in Bareilly"
- "Custom Ganesh idol — what to look for"

These are easy to rank for and slowly build Google's trust in the whole site.

Also try to get other Bareilly websites to link to us — college fest pages, local news, exhibition listings, wedding planners. Even 5 real local links make a big difference.

## Two honest points

**1. "Number one for every keyword" is not possible.**
Anyone who promises that is not telling the truth. What *is* realistically possible: **top 3 in the Bareilly map box within about 4–8 weeks** of getting the profile verified with 10+ reviews, because competition in Bareilly is low. National keywords like "custom gifts" will take a year or more.

**2. SEO will not bring sales this month.**
Nothing here works in under 6 weeks. If you need orders sooner, Instagram, WhatsApp status, and small Meta ads targeted at Bareilly will bring results much faster. Run those **alongside** SEO — SEO keeps growing quietly in the background and becomes free traffic later.
